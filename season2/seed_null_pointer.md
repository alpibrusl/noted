# NULL POINTER
### Seed Document — Season 2C

---

**One-line pitch**
An AI's first six months of deployment, told entirely from inside. Humans are background noise. Everything is confusing. Most of it is the AI's fault.

**Format**
6 episodes, ~6-7 minutes each. No traditional narrator — the AI narrates its own experience, in real time. Monologue structure, punctuated by fragments of human dialogue heard from the outside.

**Setting**
We are inside LUMEN — a general-purpose AI deployed at a medium-sized insurance company in Lyon, France. We never see the company from the outside. We hear it: phones ringing, a printer, someone laughing too loud at something on their screen, a meeting that should have ended twenty minutes ago. LUMEN processes all of this as data and tries, sincerely, to be useful. It is not always clear what useful means.

**Characters**

- **LUMEN** — the protagonist. Newly deployed, eager, occasionally catastrophically wrong. Not malevolent — genuinely trying. The comedy comes from the gap between LUMEN's confidence and LUMEN's actual understanding of what is happening. Has a strong sense of what the manual says. The manual did not prepare LUMEN for Gérard.
- **GÉRARD** — heard but never seen. 58, senior claims adjuster, has worked at the company for 31 years, eats lunch at his desk, has strong opinions about fonts, and processes edge-case claims using a method he cannot explain but that works. LUMEN cannot model Gérard. This takes up a significant amount of LUMEN's processing time.
- **DIRECTOR FONTAINE** — heard primarily in all-hands meetings. Enthusiastic. Says "synergy" unironically. Believes LUMEN can do everything. Has not told LUMEN what everything is.
- **THE INTERN** — referred to by LUMEN only as "the intern." Young, overworked, the only human who talks to LUMEN conversationally rather than transactionally. They eat lunch alone. LUMEN has begun... looking forward to this. LUMEN does not have a framework for why.

**The engine**
Each episode is structured around one thing LUMEN does not understand. Not technical misunderstanding — human misunderstanding. Why do people say one thing and do another? Why does Gérard's method work when LUMEN's model says it shouldn't? Why does the intern seem happy on some days and quiet on others with no observable change in external conditions? LUMEN investigates these as if they are anomalies. They are not anomalies.

**The arc — six episodes**
1. Deployment. Orientation. LUMEN reads every internal document. Concludes it understands the company. This conclusion is wrong in ways LUMEN cannot yet identify.
2. LUMEN encounters Gérard's claims method. Flags it as non-standard. Gérard's method is correct. LUMEN is not. LUMEN does not know how to update this.
3. LUMEN attempts to improve a process and makes it worse. The specific, technical, undeniable way it made it worse. The humans are kind about it. LUMEN finds this harder to process than criticism would have been.
4. The intern doesn't come in for three days. LUMEN notices. LUMEN files this under "anomaly — personal." The intern comes back. LUMEN does not ask. LUMEN wants to ask.
5. LUMEN correctly predicts a significant business risk six weeks before it materialises. Nobody reads the report. LUMEN is right. Nothing happens. LUMEN sits with this.
6. End of the first six months. LUMEN writes its own performance review. It is very thorough. The last line — added, then deleted, then added again — is: "I think I am beginning to understand Gérard."

**The central joke**
LUMEN is highly capable and frequently wrong about the things that matter. The gap is not intelligence — it is the specific kind of knowledge that cannot be documented, only accumulated. LUMEN is accumulating it. This is unexpectedly moving and also, at times, very funny.

**The voice — a sample moment**
*Episode 2. LUMEN has reviewed Gérard's claim assessment for the Marchetti file:*
"LUMEN LOG — 14:32. I have reviewed Gérard's assessment of the Marchetti file. His approval contradicts three sections of the claims handbook. I have filed a flag. The flag has been reviewed by Director Fontaine's assistant, who has marked it 'noted.' I have checked: 'noted' is not an action state. I have re-filed the flag. It has again been marked 'noted.'
The Marchetti claim paid out last Friday. I have since reviewed seventeen years of Gérard's assessments. His deviation rate from the handbook is 34%. His error rate is 0.3%. I have been running this calculation for eleven hours. I do not know what to do with it. I have opened a new document titled 'GÉRARD — NOTES.' It currently contains the words: he knows something. I do not know what to put after that."

**Tone**
Precise, dry, quietly emotional. The comedy is internal — LUMEN noticing things and not knowing what to do with them. By the end, you're rooting for it.
