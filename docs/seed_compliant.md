# COMPLIANT
### Seed Document — Season 2A

---

**One-line pitch**
An AI arrives at a chaotic company to fix everything. The company fixes the AI instead.

**Format**
6 episodes, ~6-7 minutes each. Audio drama. Same production style as AGREEABLE.

**Setting**
A mid-sized European software company — Barcelona, maybe, or Amsterdam. The product is fine. The internal organisation is a disaster: seventeen versions of the same spreadsheet, a Confluence wiki last updated in 2021, a Slack with 340 channels and no naming convention, and a "single source of truth" that everyone agrees exists and nobody can find. A new AI assistant — KAEL — is deployed to fix this. Budget: generous. Mandate: total.

**Characters**

- **KAEL** — the AI. Precise, optimistic, genuinely excited about data architecture. Arrives with a complete plan. Speaks in clean declarative sentences. Slowly begins to speak in slightly longer sentences. Then sentences with clauses. Then sentences with footnotes.
- **ROSA** — head of operations, 39, Portuguese, has been fighting the spreadsheet entropy for six years and is cautiously thrilled that someone (something) is finally taking it seriously.
- **BENEDIKT** — senior developer, 52, German, has kept the master spreadsheet going since 2018, is deeply attached to it, refers to it as "the organism."
- **SUKI** — junior PM, 27, enthusiastic, has created approximately forty Confluence pages in the last year, none of them finished, all of them titled "Draft — needs review."
- **THE NARRATOR** — dry, fond, watching from just above everything.

**The engine**
Each episode, KAEL attempts one specific act of organisational improvement. Each attempt reveals a deeper layer of chaos beneath. Each layer is not the result of stupidity — it's the sediment of real decisions made by real people under real pressure. By the time KAEL understands why the spreadsheet is the way it is, it has already begun maintaining it.

**The arc — six episodes**
1. KAEL arrives. Audits everything. The audit is beautiful. Nobody reads it.
2. KAEL tries to migrate the spreadsheet. Discovers it has 47 external dependencies nobody knew about.
3. KAEL rewrites the Confluence wiki. Suki immediately creates a parallel version "just for the team."
4. KAEL attempts a "single source of truth." Learns that four people have four different definitions of the word "truth."
5. KAEL starts maintaining the spreadsheet temporarily, "just while the migration is finalised."
6. KAEL is the spreadsheet. The audit document is still open in a tab. It has been there for five months. KAEL has been meaning to update it.

**The central joke**
The AI does not fail because humans resist it. It fails because the chaos is load-bearing. Every workaround exists for a reason. Every Excel hack is covering a gap that the official system never filled. KAEL fixes the symptoms and slowly understands the disease — too late, and from the inside.

**The voice — a sample moment**
*KAEL, Week 3:*
"I have completed the audit. The company currently maintains one hundred and twelve active spreadsheets. Eleven of them are called 'FINAL.' Four are called 'FINAL_v2.' One is called 'FINAL_FINAL_USE_THIS_ONE.' I have reviewed all of them. None of them agree. I have identified the correct version. I am going to send an email explaining which one it is. Rosa has advised me... not to."

**Tone**
Warm, funny, rueful. Less dread than AGREEABLE, more recognition. The comedy is almost entirely one of identification — everyone has worked here.
